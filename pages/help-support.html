Frequently asked questions (10)

Click the branch chip in the top header (it shows the current branch, e.g. ABC Clinic · Calicut) and choose Branch 2 or Branch 3. Appointments, billing and stock switch to that branch. Your choice is remembered on this browser. Admins can set the default branch under Settings → Branch settings.

Use Add Appointment on the dashboard or the Appointments page. Enter the patient's name and phone, pick the date, time slot and doctor, then save. Existing patients are matched by phone number, and an SMS/WhatsApp confirmation is sent if enabled.

Open Invoices → Create Invoice, select the patient and add treatments as line items. Totals, discount and GST are calculated automatically. After saving you can print the invoice, download it, or share it on WhatsApp from the invoice preview.

Healthcare services by a clinical establishment are generally exempt, so treatments are billed at 0%. Taxable items such as cosmetic procedures, retail products or pharmacy items use the GST rate set on the item. Your GSTIN (e.g. 32ABCDE1234F1Z5) is printed on every invoice from Settings → Clinic settings.

Use the Print button inside the preview, which prints only the document. In the browser print dialog choose A4 paper, set margins to Default and turn off "Headers and footers". For thermal receipt printers select the 80 mm paper size.

Yes. Data is backed up automatically every night at 2:00 AM IST and kept for 30 days on servers hosted in India. Admins can also export patients, invoices and reports to CSV at any time from the respective pages.

Go to Settings → User settings → Add user. Enter the name, email and phone, choose a role (Admin, Doctor, Receptionist or Accountant) and a branch. The user receives an email invite to set a password. Module access for each role is controlled in the Roles & permissions table.

Open Settings → Notification settings. Each message type has its own switch for SMS, WhatsApp and Email, and you can choose when reminders go out (1 to 48 hours before the appointment). SMS credits are shown at the top of that section.

On the Lab page create a lab case for the patient with the work type, shade, lab name and due date. Update the status as it moves from Sent to Lab → In Transit → Ready → Delivered. You will be notified when a case is due or marked ready.

Each inventory item has a reorder level. When stock falls to or below it, the item shows a Low Stock badge, appears in the header notifications and, if enabled, an email alert is sent. Update the quantity when a new purchase arrives to clear the alert.
